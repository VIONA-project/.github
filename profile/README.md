# VIONA

### Open Industrial Orchestration

**Virtualized Industrial Orchestration & Node Automation**

An open platform for deploying and operating virtual PLCs across industrial sites.

[![Project repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Explore_the_code-VIONA--Platform-2563eb?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/VIONA-project/VIONA-Platform)
[![Documentation](https://img.shields.io/badge/Read_the_docs-Documentation-0f766e?style=for-the-badge&logo=readthedocs&logoColor=white)](https://viona-project.github.io/VIONA-Platform/)

</div>

---

VIONA is an open-source project for automation engineers, system integrators, and industrial end users. Our goal is to make it practical to manage virtual PLCs from different vendors as a fleet, with a common approach to deployment, updates, diagnostics, and access.

We are developing the platform in the open so that the people who build and operate industrial systems can help shape its architecture.
